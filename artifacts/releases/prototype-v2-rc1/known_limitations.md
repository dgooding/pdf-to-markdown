# Known Limitations — prototype-v2-rc1

- Technical pass and parity success do not guarantee perfect content fidelity for all source document styles.
- Complex charts, dense diagrams, and scan-heavy pages may still require manual review.
- Markdown output is not universally guaranteed to be pixel-identical editable reconstruction of source layout.
- OCR quality is dependent on provider availability and source image quality.
- Fidelity distribution is review-aware and should be interpreted independently from technical pass metrics.
